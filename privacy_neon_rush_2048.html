Privacy Policy

2048 Elemental Sprites (formerly 2048 Neon Rush) · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

2048 Elemental Sprites is a puzzle game that works offline. Signing in with Google is optional and is only used to back up and sync your progress. The game has no ads and no in-app purchases, and we never sell your data.

Information we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, name and email address, used to create your game account.
  • Cloud save (signed-in players): scores, level and XP, coins, medals, the sprites you’ve collected and when they were last updated.
  • App analytics (Firebase Analytics): how the app is used, such as screens opened and games played, plus device and app information and identifiers, including the Android advertising ID. This is used only to understand and improve the game, never for advertising.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): crash logs and diagnostic information about your device and the app, used to fix bugs.
  • On your device only: your saved game, best scores and settings. These stay on your phone unless you sign in.

What we don’t collect

  • Location, contacts, photos, videos, files, messages or microphone audio.
  • Payment or financial information.

How data is shared and protected

Data is processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics and Crashlytics) on our behalf, under Firebase’s privacy terms. It is encrypted in transit. Your cloud save can only be read or changed by your own signed-in account. We don’t share your data with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

  • Account and cloud save data are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • To delete your account and cloud data, follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email us. We delete it within 30 days.
  • You can also sign out at any time, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has signed in, contact us and we’ll delete the data.
