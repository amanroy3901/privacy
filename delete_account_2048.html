2048 Elemental Sprites (formerly 2048 Neon Rush) · developer: OrganizeEmail / Sayne Design

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to aman.kroy2001@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my 2048 account”.
  2. Send it from, or mention, the Google account email you used to sign in to the game.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your account and cloud data within 30 days.
Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in record (Google account ID, name and email address).
  • Your cloud save: scores, level and XP, coins, medals and the sprites you’ve collected.

What is kept

  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports (not linked to your name or email) are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Game data stored only on your phone stays there until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Only want to stop syncing?

You can sign out at any time from the Profile screen in the game. The game keeps working offline without an account.
