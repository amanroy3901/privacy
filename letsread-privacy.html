LetsRead Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026 Publisher: Sayne Design Contact: aman.kroy2001@gmail.com

LetsRead is an Android app for reading EPUB and PDF files that you choose to open. This policy describes the app's data handling. It applies to the Android version of LetsRead published by Sayne Design.

Data stored on your device

LetsRead stores your library metadata, reading position and history, bookmarks, highlights, notes, collections, reading settings, and PDF conversion caches locally on your device. Imported EPUB and PDF files remain at their selected storage locations; the app accesses them through Android's file picker permissions. The app does not require an account.

Network requests

Reading and PDF conversion run on your device. LetsRead does not upload your books, notes, or reading history to our servers. If you choose to install an optional OCR language pack, the app downloads that pack from raw.githubusercontent.com and verifies its integrity. That request necessarily exposes your device's IP address and ordinary request information to GitHub under its own privacy practices. English OCR data is bundled with the app.

Exports, sharing, and backups

You can export notes or reading statistics through Android's share interface and save or restore a metadata backup using Android's document picker. The destination is the app or storage provider you select. A metadata backup includes library records, notes, bookmarks, progress, collections, settings, and reading history, but does not include the original EPUB or PDF files. Your chosen provider may handle exported data under its own privacy policy.

Android system backup may include app data according to your device and Google account backup settings. You can manage system backups in Android settings. LetsRead does not operate a separate cloud sync service.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The current app does not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or account backend. It does not sell your reading data. App distribution and optional OCR downloads involve Google Play and GitHub respectively, which may process their own service data.

Retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you remove it, clear app storage, or uninstall LetsRead. You can delete individual books and annotations in the app. Any backup or export you saved outside the app must be deleted separately from the location you chose. Android system backups follow your device backup settings.

Children

LetsRead is a general reading utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes and will revise the effective date above. For privacy questions, contact aman.kroy2001@gmail.com.
